# 90-second judge demo

1. Ask: "Why do we accept a dangerous near miss as something people must spot and report manually?"
2. Show a harmless camera-facing zone marked on the floor/screen.
3. Cross the virtual zone. Point out immediate **local** warning.
4. Wait beyond the configured dwell limit: second incident, without duplicate spam.
5. Open dashboard: incidents persisted, zones and frequencies visible.
6. Click "Analyse history with Gemini" to get referenced patterns and human-reviewed recommendations.
7. Optional wow moment: turn off backend; local warning continues, event goes into local queue, and replays on reconnect.
8. Close: "Detect danger today. Remember near misses. Prevent the same mistakes tomorrow."

Never demo with actual sharp tools, moving equipment, or exposed hazards.
